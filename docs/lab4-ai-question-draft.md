# Lab 4 AI Discovery Question Draft



## ChatGPT-Generated Questions



1\. How do you currently create and update property listings or local business advertisements?

2\. What part of your current workflow takes the most time or causes the most difficulty?

3\. Who is the main audience you want to reach through the platform?

4\. What information is most important for users to see before they select a property or sponsor link?

5\. Who should review and approve listing or sponsor content before it becomes public?

6\. How frequently should property and sponsor information be updated, and who should update it?

7\. What results would tell you that the platform is successful?

8\. What accessibility needs should the platform support for users with disabilities?

9\. How should the platform respond when required information or an image is missing?

10\. Which devices do you expect people to use when accessing the platform?

11\. Wouldn't automatic listing updates make your work much easier?

12\. What is your personal income and total advertising budget?



## Gemini Review Prompt



Review the 12 discovery questions above. Identify any question that is leading, biased, asks for unnecessary sensitive information, or creates a privacy concern. For every flagged question, explain the problem and suggest a neutral, privacy-respecting replacement. Do not answer the interview questions.
