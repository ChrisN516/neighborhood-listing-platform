# AI Studio Interview Themes



| Theme | Summary | Supporting Source Notes |

|---|---|---|

| Multi-Channel Publishing Workflows | Both stakeholders manage and distribute information across multiple separate channels, including email and websites, alongside specialized tools or outlets—a listing system and spreadsheets for the property professional, and social media and local advertising for the sponsor. | Property Professional #1; Local Sponsor #1 |

| Data Consistency and Redundancy Challenges | Both participants encounter difficulties when managing content across multiple platforms, specifically entering information in several places, maintaining consistent details such as descriptions, images, services, and links, and preventing outdated prices or availability. | Property Professional #2; Local Sponsor #2 |

| Shared Local and Relocating Audience | Both participants target overlapping local audiences: renters, people relocating or preparing to move, and local community members such as local home buyers, nearby residents, and new homeowners. | Property Professional #3; Local Sponsor #3 |

| Structured Content, Visual Media, and Links | Both roles require publishing key descriptive details alongside imagery such as photographs or images and direct web links such as a property-details link or business-website link. | Property Professional #4; Local Sponsor #4 |

| Pre-Publication Review and Approval | Both require designated authorization before content is published. The responsible listing agent reviews property information, while the business owner or an authorized manager approves sponsor information. | Property Professional #5; Local Sponsor #5 |

| Defined Information Maintenance and Update Procedures | Both establish recurring maintenance routines to keep data current, including daily checks for property price and availability and monthly or event-triggered reviews for sponsor services, hours, and links. Authorized individuals manage these updates. | Property Professional #6; Local Sponsor #6 |

| Engagement and Accuracy Success Metrics | Both evaluate success through higher audience interaction, including customer inquiries, page or website visits, qualified inquiries, and local referrals, as well as greater data accuracy through fewer outdated listings, faster updates, and accurate business information. | Property Professional #7; Local Sponsor #7 |

| Accessibility and Mobile Usability Standards | Both require universal design and usability features, specifically mobile-friendly layouts, image descriptions, keyboard navigation, accessible links, readable text and contrast, clear labels, and plain language. | Property Professional #8; Local Sponsor #8 |
