# Lab 4 Content Approval Workflow



This workflow applies to property listings and local sponsor content before and after publication.



## Roles



- Submitter: The property professional or local sponsor who provides the content.

- Reviewer: A team member who checks completeness, accuracy, links, images, and accessibility.

- Approver: The listing agent for property content or an authorized business owner or manager for sponsor content.

- Publisher: The team member responsible for making approved content public.



## Workflow



### 1. Draft



The submitter enters the required information. The content remains private and editable.



### 2. Review



The reviewer checks that required fields are complete, facts are consistent, links work, images include descriptions, and no unnecessary personal information is included.



If information is missing or incorrect, the content returns to Draft with a clear explanation.



### 3. Approval



The appropriate approver reviews the corrected content.



- A listing agent approves property content.

- A business owner or authorized manager approves sponsor content.



The approver may approve the content or return it for changes.



### 4. Publication



Only approved content may be published. The publisher records the approval and publication date.



### 5. Updates



Property professionals should review prices and listing statuses regularly. Local sponsors should review business information monthly or whenever services, hours, or links change.



Updated content follows the same review and approval process before replacing the public version.



### 6. Removal or Correction



Incorrect, expired, inaccessible, or unauthorized content should be hidden from public view. The responsible submitter is notified and must provide corrected information before republication.



## Accountability



Every content item should identify its submitter, reviewer, approver, current status, and most recent update date. Team members should use the GitHub Project stages Backlog, Ready, In Progress, Review, and Done to track the work.
