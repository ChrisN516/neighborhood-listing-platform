# Lab 4 Stakeholder Discovery Package



## Step 1: Stakeholder Groups



### Known Facts



- The platform is a neighborhood property-listing application.

- The platform displays property information for potential buyers or renters.

- The platform includes local sponsor information alongside property listings.

- The two required stakeholder groups are property professionals and local sponsors.



### Stakeholder Group 1: Property Professional



Examples include real estate agents, brokers, and property managers.



#### Assumptions to Validate



- Property professionals want a simple way to add and update listings.

- They need property prices, availability, and details to remain accurate.

- They want potential customers to find listings quickly.

- They may need to approve listing content before it becomes public.

- They want the platform to work well on both computers and mobile devices.



### Stakeholder Group 2: Local Sponsor



Examples include local restaurants, repair services, landscaping companies, and home inspectors.



#### Assumptions to Validate



- Local sponsors want their businesses shown near relevant property listings.

- They want control over their business name, description, image, and website link.

- They want to know whether users are viewing or clicking their advertisements.

- They may require approval before their business information is published.

- They want advertisements to be accessible and easy to understand.



## Step 3: Final Neutral Discovery Questions



1\. How do you currently create and update property listings or local business advertisements?

2\. What part of your current workflow takes the most time or causes the most difficulty?

3\. Who is the main audience you want to reach through the platform?

4\. What information is most important for users to see before they select a property or sponsor link?

5\. Who should review and approve listing or sponsor content before it becomes public?

6\. How frequently should property and sponsor information be updated, and who should update it?

7\. What results would tell you that the platform is successful?

8\. What accessibility needs should the platform support for users with disabilities?
