Here is the review of the 12 discovery questions, identifying any issues related to leading phrasing, bias, unnecessary sensitive information, or privacy concerns, along with neutral, privacy-respecting replacements.



Flagged Questions and Recommendations

Question 11: "Wouldn't automatic listing updates make your work much easier?"

Problem: This is a leading question. It nudges the respondent toward a specific answer by assuming automation is desirable, which can bias feedback and mask potential concerns they might have about automated workflows.



Suggested Replacement: "What are your thoughts on having automated listing updates compared to managing them manually?"



Question 12: "What is your personal income and total advertising budget?"

Problem: This question asks for unnecessary sensitive/financial information and creates a privacy concern early in a discovery process. Asking for personal income is irrelevant to platform utility, and detailed advertising budgets can make respondents guarded.



Suggested Replacement: "How do you typically allocate resources for marketing or platform features like advertising?"



Questions Approved as Neutral and Effective

The remaining questions (1 through 10) are well-crafted, open-ended, and maintain a neutral stance suitable for effective user discovery:



Question 1: "How do you currently create and update property listings or local business advertisements?" (Effectively explores current workflows).



Question 2: "What part of your current workflow takes the most time or causes the most difficulty?" (Identifies pain points without bias).



Question 3: "Who is the main audience you want to reach through the platform?" (Clarifies target demographics neutrally).



Question 4: "What information is most important for users to see before they select a property or sponsor link?" (Focuses on user needs and content priority).



Question 5: "Who should review and approve listing or sponsor content before it becomes public?" (Addresses governance without assumptions).



Question 6: "How frequently should property and sponsor information be updated, and who should update it?" (Addresses operational cadence neutrally).



Question 7: "What results would tell you that the platform is successful?" (Establishes clear success metrics).



Question 8: "What accessibility needs should the platform support for users with disabilities?" (Promotes inclusive design).



Question 9: "How should the platform respond when required information or an image is missing?" (Explores system edge cases effectively).



Question 10: "Which devices do you expect people to use when accessing the platform?" (Gathers technical context without leading the user).
