# Lab 4 Effort Estimates and Week 8 MVP



These are initial planning estimates for team review. They may change after the team discusses technical complexity and available time.



## Estimate Meanings



- Small: Limited work with few dependencies.

- Medium: Several tasks or components are required.

- Large: Significant development, testing, or multiple dependencies are required.



## User Story Estimates



| Story | Description | Estimate | Week 8 MVP |

|---|---|---|---|

| US-01 | Create a property listing | Medium | Yes |

| US-02 | Update property information | Small | Yes |

| US-03 | Approve property content | Small | No |

| US-04 | Find relevant properties | Large | Yes |

| US-05 | Manage sponsor information | Medium | Yes |

| US-06 | Approve sponsor content | Small | No |

| US-07 | Access the platform | Medium | Yes |

| US-08 | Measure platform success | Medium | No |



## Proposed Week 8 MVP



The proposed Week 8 MVP includes US-01, US-02, US-04, US-05, and US-07.



These stories provide the minimum useful experience: property professionals can create and update listings, buyers and renters can find properties, sponsors can manage business information, and visitors can use the platform accessibly.



US-03, US-06, and US-08 remain in the backlog for later work because approval automation and success reporting can be added after the core platform experience is working.



## Team Review Status



These estimates and MVP choices are proposals. The team should review them through the GitHub planning issue and update them if the workload or priorities change.
