# Lab 4 Evidence-Based Personas



These personas are working hypotheses based on simulated interview evidence. They do not represent real individuals and make no assumptions about age, gender, ethnicity, income, disability status, or technical ability. They must be revised when approved stakeholder evidence becomes available.



## Persona 1: Property Listing Professional



**Status:** Evidence-based working hypothesis



**Role:** A professional responsible for publishing and maintaining residential property listings.



**Main Goal:** Publish accurate property information quickly and help potential buyers or renters reach the correct property-details page.



**Current Tasks:**



- Enter listing information into multiple systems.

- Update prices and availability.

- Review property details and photographs.

- Approve information before publication.

- Respond to qualified customer inquiries.



**Pain Points:**



- Repeating the same data entry across several platforms.

- Discovering outdated prices or listing availability.

- Keeping property details consistent.

- Spending extra time correcting inaccurate information.



**Platform Needs:**



- One structured location for property information.

- Required fields for price, address, status, photographs, bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage.

- A review and approval step before publication.

- Fast updates for price and availability.

- Keyboard navigation, image descriptions, readable contrast, clear labels, and mobile support.



**Success Measures:**



- Fewer outdated listings.

- Faster listing updates.

- More qualified inquiries.

- More visits to property-details pages.



**Supporting Evidence:** Property Professional notes #1–#8.



**Unknowns to Validate:** Exact approval roles, required update frequency, and which existing listing systems must connect to the platform.



## Persona 2: Local Sponsor Representative



**Status:** Evidence-based working hypothesis



**Role:** A business owner or authorized manager responsible for local sponsor information.



**Main Goal:** Present accurate business information to nearby residents, renters, home buyers, and new homeowners.



**Current Tasks:**



- Publish business information across websites, social media, email, and local advertising.

- Maintain descriptions, images, service details, hours, and website links.

- Review sponsor content before publication.

- Track customer visits, inquiries, and referrals.



**Pain Points:**



- Keeping information consistent across different platforms.

- Correcting outdated services, hours, or links.

- Controlling how the business is presented.

- Understanding whether sponsor information reaches the intended audience.



**Platform Needs:**



- Structured fields for business name, category, description, service area, image, hours, and website link.

- An owner or authorized-manager approval step.

- Monthly and event-triggered update options.

- Plain language, readable text, image descriptions, keyboard-accessible links, and mobile support.



**Success Measures:**



- More website visits.

- More customer inquiries and local referrals.

- Accurate and current business information.



**Supporting Evidence:** Local Sponsor notes #1–#8.



**Unknowns to Validate:** Exact sponsor approval process, preferred performance measurements, and how frequently each business wants reminders to review its information.
