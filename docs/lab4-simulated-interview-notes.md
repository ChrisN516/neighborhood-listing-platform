# Lab 4 Simulated Interview Notes



## Interview Status



These are clearly labeled simulated interviews created for the class project because approval for real participant outreach has not been documented. No real person was contacted, and no personal or sensitive information is included.



## Simulated Interview A: Property Professional



**Role:** Real estate listing agent  

**Identity:** Simulated and de-identified



1\. **Current workflow:** Uses a listing system, spreadsheets, email, and separate websites to publish and update property information.

2\. **Main difficulty:** Entering the same information in multiple places and finding outdated prices or availability.

3\. **Main audience:** Local home buyers, renters, and people relocating to the area.

4\. **Important information:** Price, address, listing status, photographs, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and a details link.

5\. **Content approval:** The responsible listing agent should review the information before publication.

6\. **Update process:** Price and availability should be checked daily. The assigned agent should make or approve changes.

7\. **Success measures:** Fewer outdated listings, faster updates, more qualified inquiries, and more visits to property-detail pages.

8\. **Accessibility needs:** Keyboard navigation, descriptive image text, readable contrast, clear labels, and a mobile-friendly layout.



## Simulated Interview B: Local Sponsor



**Role:** Owner of a local home-services business  

**Identity:** Simulated and de-identified



1\. **Current workflow:** Shares business information through social media, a website, email, and local advertising.

2\. **Main difficulty:** Keeping business descriptions, images, service details, and links consistent across different platforms.

3\. **Main audience:** Nearby residents, new homeowners, renters, and people preparing to move.

4\. **Important information:** Business name, service category, short description, service area, image, hours, and website link.

5\. **Content approval:** The business owner or an authorized manager should approve the sponsor information before publication.

6\. **Update process:** Information should be reviewed monthly and whenever services, hours, or links change.

7\. **Success measures:** More website visits, customer inquiries, local referrals, and accurate business information.

8\. **Accessibility needs:** Plain language, readable text, image descriptions, keyboard-accessible links, and a mobile-friendly layout.



## Note-Taking and Privacy Statement



Only operational needs related to the platform were recorded. The notes contain no names, phone numbers, email addresses, financial details, or other sensitive personal information.
